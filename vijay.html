Deverakonda Vijay Sai, known professionally as Vijay Deverakonda, is an Indian actor and film producer predominantly working in Telugu cinema. He gained prominence with Pelli Choopulu and the cult success Arjun Reddy, earning accolades including a Filmfare Award, a Nandi Award, and three SIIMA Awards. His filmography features hits like Mahanati, Geetha Govindam, and Taxiwaala, as well as ventures into Hindi and Tamil films such as Liger and NOTA. He founded King of the Hill Entertainment, launched the fashion brand Rowdy Wear, and co-owns Hyderabad Black Hawks
